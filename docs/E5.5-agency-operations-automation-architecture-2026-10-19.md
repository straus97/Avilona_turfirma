**Date:** 2026-10-19 · **Slice:** E5.5-A0 (documentation only) · **Baseline:** application `c56e3306`, docs `c68b6814` (E5 CLOSED, unchanged)

> **Status: ARCHITECTURE DECISION. Nothing in this document is implemented.** No code, migration, route, policy, UI or test was added by E5.5-A0. Every "will", "target" and "AOA-NN" below is a plan. ## 2. Accepted architecture

| Object | Role | Change |
|---|---|---|
| `IncomingInquiry` | Tourvisor **technical intake and provenance** (import state, vendor snapshot, webhook idempotency) | Stays as is. Not the business inquiry. |
| **`Inquiry`** (new) | Provider-independent **business** entity: any source, owner, customer link, lifecycle | New in AOA-03. |
| `Booking` | Existing **Avilona operational booking** (customer-facing lifecycle, chat, documents) | Stays. Created from Inquiry by explicit conversion. |
| **`OperatorBooking`** (new) | The **actual booking at the tour operator**, with provenance, import snapshots, diff, approval | New in AOA-06. |
| Customer receipts ledger / operator payments ledger (new) | Separate durable, auditable money records | New in AOA-09 / AOA-10. |

```text
IncomingInquiry (Tourvisor provenance) --links to--> Inquiry <-- manual / VK / email / phone / office / referral / other
Inquiry --explicit conversion, at most one--> Booking --0..n--> OperatorBooking
Booking --> customer receipts ledger            OperatorBooking --> operator payments ledger
```

Indicative only (schema is decided inside each slice, not here): Inquiry carries `source_channel`, creator, owner, optional explicit customer link, optional `incoming_inquiry` provenance link and optional unique `booking_id`. Whether one Booking may have several OperatorBookings must not be precluded by the schema; cardinality is confirmed in AOA-06.

No dependency on U-ON, Qui-Quo or any external CRM.

## 3. Business invariants (authoritative for all AOA slices)

**Inquiry and ownership**

1. Inquiry may originate from Tourvisor, VK, email, messenger, phone, office, referral, manual or other sources.
2. A Manager-created Inquiry belongs to its creator automatically.
3. An Admin-created Inquiry belongs to the Admin by default, or to an explicitly selected eligible employee.
4. An automatically received, unassigned Inquiry is allocated by Admin (self-assignment allowed). A Manager cannot independently claim it. Admin may reassign.
5. Customer selection is explicit. Contact similarity (e-mail, phone, name) never proves identity.
6. Inquiry and Booking are distinct lifecycle objects. Explicit conversion creates **at most one** native Booking and keeps the E5 guarantees (transactional, row-locked, re-checked, idempotent, provenance retained).

**Operator side**

7. Operator bookings are made **manually by agency staff on the operator's own portal**. Avilona never purchases or books at an operator automatically.
8. Actual operator booking data lives in the separate OperatorBooking domain: provenance, import snapshots, diff, controlled approval.
9. Operator access priority: officially permitted API → first-party Avilona Bridge adapter where authorised → manual entry.
10. Bridge is a thin, staff-operated browser connector, **not another CRM**. It never stores operator passwords, never bypasses CAPTCHA, never applies silent financial updates.

**Money**

11. The operator's displayed/base selling price **already includes** the agency commission. Commission is never added on top of the customer's price.
12. Client final price = confirmed base customer price − approved customer discount, subject to a future explicit business policy. Commission and operator settlement are separate internal concepts.
13. Foreign currency, the operator-specific exchange rate and the confirmed RUB amount are tracked as entered/confirmed. **No substitution with Central Bank rates.**
14. Commission, margin, operator settlements and internal profit are **never** serialised or exposed in Tourist/client documents, chat or outbound correspondence through automated data paths (allow-list serialisation + tests, not "remember not to").
15. Customer receipts and operator payments are separate, durable, auditable ledgers. `bookings.total_price` / `bookings.paid_amount` remain **compatibility projections** until a deliberate migration says otherwise.
16. Booking lifecycle status, operator status, client payment status, operator settlement and document readiness are **independent** dimensions. No payment-blocking business rule is introduced without owner approval (AO-09).

**Documents and communications**

17. Documents use approved templates, immutable revisions, secure storage and **explicit client publication**. Operator-issued documents stay operator-issued (never re-authored as Avilona's).
18. Deadlines, a limited template set, VK community messages and outbound e-mail arrive incrementally.

**Platform**

19. Existing passwords/security hardening, policies and user accounts remain intact.
20. Production stays on REG.RU; the Timeweb server is preordered and is **not** production. No deployment is part of E5.5-A0.
21. Canonical MySQL `turfirma_rebuild_v4` is never altered automatically. Every AOA migration is additive, tested on SQLite `:memory:`, and applied to canonical/production only through a separate guarded plan.

### 4.1 Scope

- **First release (core operations):** AOA-01 → AOA-10 — audit foundation, visibility policy, Inquiry entity/manual workflow/allocation/conversion, OperatorBooking with manual entry and diff/approval, money model and the two ledgers — followed by an AOA-18-style integrated QA gate.

This split is a recommendation and may be re-cut by the owner; the dependency column is the binding part.

## 5. Guards for every AOA slice

- Documentation-first approval of the slice's exact allowed paths; one semantic slice at a time; no dependency upgrades mixed in.
- Additive migrations only; tests on SQLite `:memory:`; no canonical MySQL connection or write; no migration applied outside a guarded plan.
- No Tourvisor/VK/operator/e-mail provider calls in tests or development without explicit operational approval.
- No secrets, operator credentials or personal data in the repository, fixtures, logs or documentation.
- Internal-only money fields excluded from client-facing serialisation by an allow-list, with a regression test per new client-facing surface.
- Existing authentication, password hardening and policies are not weakened.
